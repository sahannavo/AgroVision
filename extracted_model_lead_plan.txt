Model context MODEL CONTEXT (paste after the common rules for every task) PURPOSE: an image classifier that runs on the phone with TensorFlow Lite and tells the farmer what is wrong with one leaf: a disease, a pest problem, a nutrient deficiency, or healthy. It works fully offline. CLASSES (the label contract): - ml/labels_schema.md is the single list of labels. Format: &lt;Crop&gt;___&lt;Condition&gt; with three underscores, for example Tomato___Early_blight, Tomato___healthy, Potato___Late_blight, Pepper___Bacterial_spot. The label text never changes after the freeze on Thu 19 Nov. - Version 1 (baseline) covers the PlantVillage classes for tomato (10), potato (3) and bell pepper (2): 15 classes. The app crop &quot;Chilli&quot; uses the Pepper labels for now; say this limit in the model card. - Version 2 adds, if the data allows: chilli leaf diseases, eggplant (brinjal) leaf diseases and nutrient deficiencies (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium) in tomato and chilli leaves, from public data sets that MASN finds, plus the team&apos;s own phone photos. FILES THAT THE APP READS (the hand-over contract): - app/assets/model/model.tflite: the model. It must not be empty. - app/assets/model/labels.txt: one label per line; the line number is the model output index (first line = index 0). It must match ml/labels_schema.md exactly. - app/assets/data/disease_catalog.json: the farmer-facing text for every label (name, description, action steps, prevention). A unit test fails if a label has no entry. - ml/README.md: the exact preprocessing the app must repeat (input size, colour order, scaling, orientation) and the command to rebuild the model. - ml/testdata/: 3 to 5 sample leaf images with the expected top-3 labels and probabilities from the TFLite model, as ml/testdata/expected.json. SDL&apos;s test in the app must reproduce these results, which proves the app and the training scripts preprocess images the same way. CONFIDENCE: the app shows &quot;not sure&quot; below the named constant kConfidenceThreshold (0.70). The evaluation says whether 0.70 is a good value. TARGETS (aims, not promises): model under 10 MB, inference under 1 second on a mid-range phone, a per-class report and a confusion matrix. A lab-photo accuracy is not the same as field accuracy: the test set must include real phone photos taken by the team. DATA RULES: PlantVillage images are lab photos on a plain background, so train with strong augmentation (rotation, flip, brightness, contrast, zoom, blur, background variation). No image, and no near-duplicate of an image, may appear in two of train, validation and test. The test split includes the team&apos;s phone photos and is never used for training or tuning. Check every data set&apos;s licence and record it. FALLBACK RULES: (1) The baseline v1 must be committed on dev by Mon 16 Nov 21:00, even if rough. (2) Version 2 replaces v1 only if it is better on the test set AND on the team&apos;s phone photos AND the catalog test passes, and only before Thu 19 Nov 18:00. (3) After the freeze the model files do not change.
